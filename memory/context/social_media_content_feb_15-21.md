## NOTES & BEST PRACTICES

1. **Alt Text**: Always include descriptive alt text for accessibility
2. **Hashtags**: Rotate hashtags to avoid shadowban; max 30 on Instagram
3. **Timing**: Post during peak hours (10-11 AM, 6-7 PM CET)
4. **Engagement**: Respond to comments within 2 hours for algorithm boost
5. **Cross-posting**: Tailor content for each platform (don't just duplicate)
6. **Analytics**: Review performance daily; adjust strategy weekly
7. **Brand Voice**: Friendly, authentic, empowering - no aggressive sales tactics
8. **Visuals**: High quality only; consistent brand aesthetic
9. **CTAs**: Clear and specific; always include link
10. **Community**: Feature UGC when possible; build relationships, not just followers
