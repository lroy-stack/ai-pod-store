# Review Response Templates

## Positive Review (4-5 stars)
Thank you so much for your kind words, {name}! We're thrilled that you love your {product}. Your support means the world to us!

## Negative Review (1-2 stars)
We're sorry to hear about your experience, {name}. This isn't the quality we strive for. We'd love to make it right — please reach out to us at {support_email} and we'll find a solution together.

## Neutral Review (3 stars)
Thank you for your honest feedback, {name}! We appreciate you taking the time to share your thoughts. We're always working to improve — your input helps us get better.

## Notes
- Always personalize with customer name and product
- Match the customer's language
- Keep brand voice from SOUL.md
