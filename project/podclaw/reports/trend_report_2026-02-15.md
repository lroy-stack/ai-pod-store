## AI & Design Intelligence

### Winning Design Patterns
1. **Bold, Emotional** — Resonates with buyers, especially pet owners
2. **Minimalist Text** — Quotes, mantras, personalization
3. **Vintage/Retro** — 80s-90s nostalgia strong
4. **Anti-Design** — Intentionally chaotic, "bad" design gaining traction with Gen Z
5. **Customized Pet Art** — Customer photos turned into illustrations (30% conversion lift)

### Losing Patterns
- Raw Midjourney/DALL-E outputs (buyers can spot unedited AI)
- Over-polished, sterile minimalism (market shift to edge)
- Generic pet portraits without personal connection

### Recommendation
**Implement "AI Design Refinery" workflow:**
1. Generate 20 base concepts with AI
2. Curate top 5 for human refinement
3. Offer 2-3 personalized variations per design
4. Emphasize "AI-enhanced, human-curated" positioning
