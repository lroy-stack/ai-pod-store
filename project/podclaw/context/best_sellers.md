## AI-Generated Design Trends 🤖

### What's Working in 2026
- ✅ **Bold, Emotional Designs** — Resonating strongly with buyers
- ✅ **Minimalist Text** — Quotes, mantras, personal messages
- ✅ **Vintage/Retro Revival** — 80s-90s nostalgia continues
- ✅ **AI + Human Curation** — Refined, edited AI art (NOT raw outputs)
- ✅ **Anti-Design Movement** — Chaotic, "intentionally bad" designs gaining traction

### What's NOT Working
- ❌ Mass-generated AI art (Midjourney/DALL-E defaults)
- ❌ Clean, over-polished minimalism (market shifting toward edge)
- ❌ Generic AI-generated pet portraits without personalization
- ❌ Unedited/unrefined outputs
