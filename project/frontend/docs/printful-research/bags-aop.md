## 1. All-Over Print Duffle Bag

### Product Info
- **Full name**: All-Over Print Duffle Bag
- **Brand / Manufacturer**: Printful (blank sourced from Sublicolor, model 606A — components sourced from China)
- **Printful Catalog ID**: **465**
- **Printful public URL**: https://www.printful.com/custom/bags/backpacks/all-over-print-duffle-bag
- **Physical dimensions**: 22″ × 11.5″ × 11.5″ (55.9 × 29.2 × 29.2 cm)
- **Volume / weight capacity**: not specified on public page; large capacity (multiple pairs of shoes + items per customer reviews)
- **Fabric weight**: 9.56 oz/yd² (325 g/m²), may vary by ±5%

### EU Fulfillment
- **EU fulfillment**: UNCERTAIN — Printful fulfills 481+ products from EU centers (Latvia/Riga and Spain/Barcelona), but bags with AOP sublimation are cut-and-sew products. The blank components are **sourced from China** (confirmed from Swaggy Catalog spec sheet). Whether final sublimation + assembly happens at the Riga EU facility for EU orders is NOT confirmed from public data. Requires verification in the Printful dashboard under "Where is this product fulfilled?" for EU customers.
- **EU fulfillment centers**: Riga, Latvia (Lidostas Parks, near Riga Int'l Airport) and Barcelona, Spain
- **GPSR note**: Printful acts as EU Authorised Representative for all products they fulfill, providing GPSR compliance documentation. The blank origin (China) means the product itself is not EU-made — verify GPSR responsible person chain before listing for EU customers.
- **Customs**: Orders fulfilled in EU are exempt from import customs duties within EU member states.

### Print Technique & Areas
- **Technique**: All-Over Print (AOP) sublimation — design is printed on transfer paper, then heat-pressed and fused into the polyester fabric, then cut-and-sewn
- **Print areas**: The duffle bag has **multiple separate print panels**. Confirmed printable areas from Printful's own product listing:
  - Front panel
  - Back panel
  - Bottom panel
  - Top panel
  - Side/mesh pocket area
  - Inside label (branding)
- **Number of print files**: Up to 6 separate design files (one per panel). Some sources indicate 7 customizable sides total including the inside label.
- **Print file format**: The fabric construction (cut-and-sew sublimation) means each panel is printed and then assembled by hand — a "unique, handmade product" per Printful's description.

### Design Requirements (DPI, dimensions, format)
- **DPI**: 150 DPI minimum (standard for all Printful AOP products). 300 DPI recommended for maximum sharpness.
- **File format**: PNG (recommended — supports transparency) or JPEG (for designs covering the full panel without transparent areas)
- **Color profile**: sRGB (IEC61966-2.1)
- **Maximum file size**: 200 MB per file
- **Maximum pixel dimensions**: 20,000 px on any side
- **Pixel dimensions per panel**: NOT publicly documented — each panel has different dimensions based on the physical cut pattern. Must download the PSD/PDF template from the "File guidelines" tab on the Printful product page (requires Printful account login).
- **Estimated print area (front panel)**: Based on the physical dimensions (22″ wide × ~11.5″ circumference front), a rough estimate at 150 DPI would be approximately **3300 × 1725 px** — but this is an approximation. Use official template.
- **Bleed area**: Include bleed extending to edges of each panel template; seam areas will be sewn and cut, so keep critical design elements inside the safe zone marked on the template.
- **Safe zone**: Keep text and logos at minimum 0.5″ from seam lines.

### Colors & Sizes
- **Sizes**: One size only (22″ × 11.5″ × 11.5″)
- **Colors**: White base (AOP sublimation requires white polyester for accurate color reproduction). Design covers the full surface; no separate "colorway" variants — the design IS the color.
- **Strap**: Adjustable and removable padded shoulder strap included (black hardware)
- **Hardware**: Dual padded handles with hook & loop fasteners; T-piping for structural stability; mesh side pocket; multiple inside pockets

### Material
- 100% polyester with black interlining
- Fabric weight: 9.56 oz/yd² (325 g/m²)
- Blank components sourced from China

### Pricing
- **Printful base price**: ~$70.50 USD (confirmed from public product page, first fetch)
- **EUR equivalent**: approximately €65–68 EUR (varies with exchange rate)
- **Suggested retail (EU)**: €109–€129 EUR for a margin of ~40–47%
- **Note**: Printful prices in USD; final EUR pricing depends on their EUR pricing at time of integration. Verify current base cost in dashboard.

### Production Time
- **Estimated**: 2–5 business days (standard for Printful AOP products)
- **Note**: Cut-and-sew AOP products may take longer than garment AOP (5–7 days is a realistic expectation for bags). Confirm on dashboard.

### Placeholders & Templates
- **Template availability**: PSD template + PDF instructions available under "File guidelines" tab on Printful product page (requires login)
- **Template structure**: Separate layers per panel (front, back, bottom, top, pocket, label) with safe zone, bleed, and fold/seam guides
- **Download**: Only accessible via authenticated Printful account > product catalog > duffle bag > File guidelines

### Notes
- Customer reviews consistently praise vibrant colors but note that coarse fabric texture means fine details (thin lines, small text) may appear slightly fuzzy — use bold, high-contrast designs
- Strap durability issues reported by some customers; plastic hardware clips noted as a potential weak point
- Print may appear slightly darker than digital preview — account for this in design
- Not suitable for EU-first strategy if blanks are sourced from China without confirmed EU-side assembly — verify fulfillment location in dashboard before adding to SKAPARA (EU-only store policy)

## 4. All-Over Print Drawstring Bag

### Product Info
- **Full name**: All-Over Print Drawstring Bag
- **Brand / Manufacturer**: Printful (blank sourced from Israel per Swaggy Catalog spec)
- **Printful Catalog ID**: **262** (confirmed via public Printful API catalog)
- **Printful public URL**: https://www.printful.com/custom/accessories/all/all-over-print-drawstring-bag (also at /custom/bags/drawstring/all-over-print-drawstring-bag)
- **Physical dimensions**: 15″ W × 17″ H (38.1 × 43.2 cm) — one size only
- **Weight capacity**: Maximum 33 lbs (15 kg)
- **Rating**: 5 stars across 199 customer reviews

### EU Fulfillment
- **EU fulfillment**: UNCERTAIN — blank components are sourced from Israel per the Swaggy Catalog product spec. This makes EU fulfillment less certain because Printful's EU centers (Latvia, Spain) primarily handle sublimation of blanks they receive — blanks from Israel may or may not be stocked at EU facilities. Requires dashboard verification.
- **GPSR note**: Blank origin is Israel — not EU. Printful acts as EU Authorised Representative regardless of blank origin. However, the product will NOT carry a "Made in EU" designation. Ensure GPSR manufacturer details reflect the Printful/Israel supply chain.
- **Shipping note**: Some outsourced Printful products have shipping restrictions to certain countries — verify this product's EU shipping eligibility in the Printful help center (article: "Are there shipping restrictions for certain outsourced products?").

### Print Technique & Areas
- **Technique**: All-Over Print (AOP) sublimation — "fabric is printed with your custom design, then cut and sewn to create a unique, handmade product" (confirmed from Swaggy Catalog spec)
- **Print areas**: The entire outer surface of the bag is printed. Likely a single large print file covering front and back as one flat piece (the bag is a simple flat-sewn construction).
- **Print file dimensions**: From Quirky Neighbor review article — **16″ × 38″ at 150 DPI = 2400 × 5700 pixels** (this is the full flat print template including front + back panels laid out flat)
- **Number of print files**: 1 file (single flat layout covering front and back panels)

### Design Requirements (DPI, dimensions, format)
- **DPI**: 150 DPI minimum (standard AOP)
- **Confirmed pixel dimensions**: **2400 × 5700 px at 150 DPI** (16″ × 38″) — sourced from Quirky Neighbor detailed review
- **File format**: PNG or JPEG
- **Color profile**: sRGB
- **Maximum file size**: 200 MB
- **Design tip**: Tileable/repeating patterns work especially well on drawstring bags due to the single-panel flat print construction. Both front and back visible simultaneously in the template.
- **Safe zone**: Keep important design elements away from the very top edge (drawstring casing area) and bottom seam.

### Colors & Sizes
- **Sizes**: One size (15″ W × 17″ H)
- **Colors**: White base only (AOP sublimation). The drawstrings/cords are typically a contrasting color (often black or white — verify in product options).
- **Construction**: Twin cotton handles (drawstrings); top drawstring closure; can be worn as a backpack

### Material
- 100% spun polyester fabric
- **Fabric weight**:
  - US product: 7.22 oz/yd² (245 g/m²)
  - EU product: 6.64 oz/yd² (225 g/m²) — note the different weight suggests EU units may use a different blank sourcing
- Blank components sourced from Israel (per Swaggy Catalog; EU weight difference suggests possible EU-specific variant)

### Pricing
- **Printful base price**: ~$18.75 USD (public product page, first fetch); Swaggy Catalog listed at $22.34 USD (reseller markup)
- **EUR equivalent**: approximately €17–19 EUR
- **Suggested retail (EU)**: €29–€34 EUR for a margin of ~40–44%
- **Note**: Lowest base cost of the 4 researched bags — good entry-level accessory product

### Production Time
- **Estimated**: 2–5 business days (standard Printful AOP)

### Placeholders & Templates
- Template: available under "File guidelines" tab on Printful product page (login required)
- **Confirmed template size**: 16″ × 38″ / 2400 × 5700 px at 150 DPI (Quirky Neighbor)
- Single-file layout (front + back flat)

### Notes
- **Best-documented print dimensions** of the 4 products (2400 × 5700 px confirmed)
- Customer reviews praise vibrant colors and thick material, but note:
  - Thin drawstrings (potential durability concern for heavy use)
  - Thick material makes closure slightly stiff
- The EU-specific fabric weight difference (225 g/m² vs 245 g/m² US) may indicate a different blank supplier for EU orders — good sign for EU availability but needs dashboard confirmation
- Lowest cost entry point — good for testing SKAPARA bag category before committing to higher-cost backpacks

## Design Recommendations for SKAPARA Brand

### Minimalist Backpack (highest priority — EU confirmed)
- **3 print areas**: front (body), top, bottom
- Front: Primary SKAPARA design (bold graphic, AOP pattern, or brand illustration)
- Top panel: SKAPARA wordmark or S mark on clean background
- Bottom panel: Subtle SKAPARA pattern or solid complementary color
- **Design style**: AOP pattern designs work best — geometric, abstract, brand-color fills

### Drawstring Bag (best entry point — lowest cost)
- **1 print file**: 2400 × 5700 px, single flat layout
- Full-wrap pattern designs ideal — repeating geometric patterns, brand illustrations
- **Brand placement**: SKAPARA wordmark on front panel center; subtle S mark pattern as background

### Duffle Bag (premium tier)
- 6 print panels — complex to design but high perceived value at ~€109–129
- Coordinate design across all 6 panels for a cohesive look
- Reserved for SKAPARA premium/travel collection
