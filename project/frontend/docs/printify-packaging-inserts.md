## What is a Packaging Insert?

Packaging inserts are additional items included with orders — from promotional materials to return labels to fragile item protection.

They function as **"mini-brand ambassadors, spreading the word about your products' quality while securing customer loyalty."**

Custom inserts typically feature brand logos, slogans, and exclusive offers, working well with custom-branded products.

### Ideas for Packaging Inserts

- **Thank-you notes:** Express gratitude and request product reviews, generating five-star ratings.
- **Store and social media links:** Provide URLs and QR codes directing customers to your online storefront and social profiles.
- **Promo codes:** Include discount codes for future purchases or to share with friends.
- **Washing and use instructions:** Provide care information for apparel and items requiring maintenance.
- **Gifts and product samples:** Reward customers with valuable additions encouraging future orders.

## Why and How to Use Packaging Inserts

### Boost Repeat Sales

Converting one-time shoppers into repeat customers is essential for eCommerce success. Thoughtfully crafted inserts play a central role by including exclusive discount codes, free samples, or loyalty program information.

### Offer Other Relevant Products

Packaging inserts present ideal cross-selling opportunities. Customers may enjoy complementary products they purchased.

- **Suggest complementary products:** Showcase items pairing well with purchases — for example, a coffee blend with a coffee mug.
- **Highlight new arrivals and bestsellers:** Spotlight top sellers or recent additions customers may not know about.
- **Offer exclusive promotions:** Provide special deals on related products, increasing customer lifetime value.

### Strengthen Your Brand Awareness

Share essential brand information including core values and mission through custom design inserts. When customers identify with your brand story, they're more likely to become repeat buyers and brand ambassadors.

### Create a Personal Connection with Customers

> **"86% of shoppers are ready to pay more for a great customer experience"** — demonstrating the value of enhanced unboxing experiences.

- **Personalized messages:** Create insert cards with warmth equivalent to handwritten notes.
- **Exclusive deals:** Express gratitude by offering distinctive benefits customers wouldn't expect.

### Add a Branded Touch

Incorporating brand identity into communication enhances customer connection through recognition and trust.

- **Your brand visuals:** Feature company logo, color scheme, and signature style.
- **Targeted language:** Use language resonating with your audience and reflecting your brand identity.

### Boost Social Media Engagement

- **Call to action:** Ask customers to share unboxing experiences on Instagram, Facebook, or X.
- **Use hashtags:** Popular hashtags provide exposure; unique branded hashtags track related posts.
- **Organize giveaways and contests:** Offer free gifts for follows, likes, shares, and prizes for winners.
- **Showcase collaborations:** Highlight partnerships with influencers or other brands.
- **Share exclusive content:** Offer behind-the-scenes footage, product sneak peeks, or special promo codes for loyal customers.

### Collect Positive Reviews

> **"95% of consumers use reviews to guide their purchasing decisions"** — directly affecting store revenue.

Showcase positive reviews and testimonials on your website to enhance trust and increase store rankings on eCommerce channels like Etsy or eBay.

- **Encourage customer feedback:** Emphasize the value of customer opinions with friendly messages requesting reviews.
- **Offer discount codes and giveaways:** Motivate reviews with rewards, emphasizing honest feedback is valued.
- **Preemptively address quality concerns:** Mention customers should contact you before leaving reviews if issues arise.

## How to Create Effective Inserts

First, understand your goals. Identify what you want to achieve, then use those goals to guide design. Keep messaging brief and avoid overwhelming details. Experiment with different designs and messaging, adjusting based on feedback and results.

### How to Design Custom Box Inserts

Add helpful information and brand design elements resonating with your audience. Strategically place your logo to maximize visibility without overshadowing your message.

### Best Practices for Making Custom Inserts

- **Add your store logo and promo codes:**
  > "56% of merchants included their store logo in custom box insert designs, and 57% added promo codes."

- **Include calls to review and shop links:**
  > "36% of merchants encouraged customers to leave reviews, while 31% also included links to their online store."

- **Add social media links, QR codes, and washing instructions:** Many merchants include these features to enhance engagement and provide helpful information.

- **Add gift messages:** If customers included gift messages with purchases, add them to package inserts for personal touches.

- **Showcase product use cases:** Show products being used by satisfied customers to inspire future orders and increase brand loyalty.

## How Do I Create Packaging Inserts with Printify?

### Step-by-Step Setup

1. Go to your **Printify dashboard**
2. Click the **top right corner menu**
3. Navigate to **Branding**
4. Select **Set up package inserts**
5. Click **Start designing**
6. Use the free graphics and design tools to create inserts matching your specifications
7. Click **Save** and enable inserts to add your product insert to all eligible orders

### Which Orders Are Eligible for Packaging Inserts?

To find products that support packaging inserts:

1. Go to **Product Catalog**
2. Select a **product category**
3. In the **left sidebar filters**, scroll to **Branding**
4. Checkmark **Inserts** to see all products offering this feature

## How to Measure the Results

### Monitor Sales and Website Traffic

Include unique QR codes and URLs in custom box inserts to track exact sales and profile view numbers. This determines whether inserts effectively drive purchases.

### Track Feedback and Interactions

Review customer feedback to gauge overall satisfaction and unboxing experience. Consider setting up a customer relationship management (CRM) system such as:

- **Agile**
- **Zoho**
- **Salesforce**

These tools provide insights into customer behavior and preferences.

### Analyze and Adjust

Revise products and packaging ideas regularly to identify what works and what doesn't. Make adjustments meeting your business goals.

You can also place orders for **product samples** via:

> Printify Dashboard → Orders → Create order → Sample

This verifies products meet expectations and packaging needs before sending to customers.
