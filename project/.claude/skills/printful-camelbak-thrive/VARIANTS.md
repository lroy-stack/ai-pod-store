## Color Details

### 1. Charcoal (Variant 22016)

- **Finish**: Dark grey/charcoal matte body
- **Design compatibility**: Excellent for light/white designs — high contrast
- **UV print notes**: White underbase renders crisp whites. Dark design elements may be less visible.
- **Recommendation**: CORE color — include in all products

### 2. Clear (Variant 22017)

- **Finish**: Transparent/translucent body
- **Design compatibility**: Unique — UV print floats on transparent surface. Liquid visible behind print.
- **UV print notes**: No solid background behind print. White underbase provides opacity for the printed area only. Unprinted areas remain see-through.
- **Recommendation**: FEATURED color — great for showcase/hero images. Eye-catching transparency effect.

### 3. Cove (Variant 22018)

- **Finish**: Light blue/teal body
- **Design compatibility**: Good for dark or vibrant designs. Avoid pale blue elements (blend with bottle).
- **UV print notes**: Transparent areas show the cove blue. White elements pop well.
- **Recommendation**: INCLUDE — appeals to ocean/water aesthetic

### 4. Forest Green (Variant 22019)

- **Finish**: Dark green body
- **Design compatibility**: Good for light/white designs — high contrast on dark base.
- **UV print notes**: Similar to Charcoal — dark base benefits from light design elements.
- **Recommendation**: INCLUDE — nature/eco positioning aligns with recycled material story

### 5. Oxford Blue (Variant 22020)

- **Finish**: Deep navy/royal blue body
- **Design compatibility**: Excellent for light/white designs — strong contrast.
- **UV print notes**: Classic dark base. White underbase ensures full color accuracy.
- **Recommendation**: CORE color — versatile, professional appearance
